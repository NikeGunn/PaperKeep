## 2. Description of Service

Paperkeep is an Android document scanning application. Everything the App does runs on your device. There is no server, no account, no cloud sync. Specifically, Paperkeep lets you:

- Capture, enhance, and store scanned documents encrypted on your device
- Run on-device OCR, document-type classification, and image enhancement
- Export documents as PDF (with searchable OCR text), JPEG, PNG, plain text, or encrypted ZIP
- Create local encrypted backups to any location you choose via Android's Storage Access Framework (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, internal storage, SD card, USB-OTG, etc.)
- Restore from a backup you previously created

### Free tier
The free tier includes all scanning, OCR, export, and backup features. It also displays occasional interstitial advertisements.

### Paperkeep Pro (one-time purchase)
Paperkeep offers a single optional upgrade called **Paperkeep Pro**, purchased once through Google Play Billing at a localized price. Pro removes advertisements, unlocks batch export of more than 5 pages at once, unlocks unlimited use of the on-device summarizer, and enables the Pro visual theme. Pro is not a subscription — there is no recurring charge.

### What Paperkeep does NOT do
Paperkeep does not provide cloud sync, server-side AI, team sharing, collaborative editing, or any account-based feature. Nothing you create with Paperkeep is uploaded to any server controlled by us.
