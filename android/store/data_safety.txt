SECTION 1: Data collection and security
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
A: NO — Paperkeep does not collect or share any user data.

Q: Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
A: N/A — no user data is transmitted.

Q: Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
A: N/A — no user data is stored on any server. Users can delete the app
   and all data is removed with it. We have no copy of any user data.

SECTION 3: AdMob disclosure (third-party SDK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Paperkeep uses Google AdMob for interstitial ads (after every 5th export).
Google's AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Device or other IDs (Advertising ID) — for ad personalisation
    Purpose: Advertising or marketing
    Required or optional: Optional (user can opt out via system settings)
    Data shared with third parties: Yes (Google)
    Processing: Ephemeral (not stored by Paperkeep)

All AdMob data handling is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
EU users are shown a GDPR UMP consent form before any ad loads.
Paperkeep Pro users (coming soon) see no ads and no ad SDK is initialised.

SECTION 5: Summary statement for Play Store
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

"No data collected" — Paperkeep does not collect, share, or transmit any
user data. All scanned documents, OCR text, and settings stay on your
device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The only network traffic is Google
AdMob (interstitial ads, subject to Google's Privacy Policy) and optional
Play Billing for the Pro upgrade.
