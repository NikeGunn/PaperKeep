# Tester recruitment — copy/paste templates

You need ≥ 12 opted-in testers before the 14-day Play closed-testing clock starts.
Each tester needs:
1. A Gmail address (to add to the Console tester list)
2. To click the opt-in URL Console gives you, on the same device they'll test on
3. To install Paperkeep from the Play Store (NOT sideload — install via the link)

## Where to find 12 testers

- 4 friends/family with Android phones (easy yes)
- 3 from r/androidapps "test my app" thread (pre-launch reviewers)
- 3 from BetaList / BetaTesting / Google Play Beta Testers FB group
- 2 from your professional network on LinkedIn or X
- Optional: post on an India-focused Android sub if you want regional feedback first

## Email/DM template (paste into Gmail or WhatsApp)

> **Subject:** Want to test my new Android scanner app? (5 min, free)
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I've been building **Paperkeep**, an Android document scanner that's a
> privacy-first alternative to CamScanner. Everything stays on your phone —
> no account, no cloud upload, no ads on the camera screen, AES-256 encryption
> on disk.
>
> I need 12 testers to start the Play Store 14-day review window. You'd:
>
> 1. Send me the **Gmail address** you use on your Android phone
> 2. Click the link I send back (opens Play Store)
> 3. Install + use it like a normal app for ~2 weeks
> 4. Tell me anything that breaks or feels off (one short message is fine)
>
> No NDA. Free forever. You'll have the app before the public launch.
>
> If you're in, just reply with your Gmail.
>
> Thanks,
> Nikhil

## After they reply with a Gmail

Add their address to the `alpha-1-testers` email list in Play Console.
Then send them this:

> Thanks! You're on the list.
>
> 1. Open this on your Android phone (must be signed into the Gmail you sent me): **[OPT-IN URL FROM PLAY CONSOLE]**
> 2. Tap "Become a tester"
> 3. The Play Store will let you install Paperkeep
>
> Tap → install → use it for whatever you'd use a scanner for. Receipts, IDs,
> notes, whiteboards, books, whatever. If something looks weird or crashes,
> screenshot it and reply to this thread. That's it.

## Day 3 follow-up

> Hey — quick check, did you get a chance to install and play with Paperkeep?
> No pressure if not, but if you hit any blockers (won't install, crashes on
> open, camera doesn't work) please let me know — that's the kind of thing I
> need to fix before the public launch.

## Day 7 focus prompt (give them something specific to look at)

> Quick favor: this week, can you try **scanning a multi-page document** (like
> a bill or receipt) and exporting it as a PDF? I want to know if the text in
> the PDF is selectable when you open it (long-press to copy a word). That's
> the OCR pipeline I'm most worried about. Reply with a yes/no, takes 30s.

## Day 12 final ask

> Last week of the closed test! If you've used Paperkeep at all, even once,
> would you mind:
>
> 1. Going to the Play Store listing
> 2. Tapping "Rate this app"
> 3. Leaving any honest rating (1-5 stars + optional comment)
>
> Even 1-star feedback is useful — I'd rather hear it now than after public
> launch. Thanks for testing!
