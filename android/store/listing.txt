App Name: ScanVault — Document Scanner

Short Description (80 chars max):
Scan, organise and export documents. Private, on-device, no subscription.

Full Description:
ScanVault is a powerful document scanner that keeps your files private and on your device — no forced cloud accounts, no subscriptions, no ads on core features.

FEATURES
• Smart edge detection — auto-detects document corners in real time
• Five capture modes — Document, Receipt, ID Card, Whiteboard, Book Scan
• OCR (text recognition) — search inside scanned documents
• PDF export with invisible text layer — fully searchable PDFs
• Image filters — Original, Auto, Magic Color, Grayscale, Black & White
• Signature tool — draw, save and place your signature on any document
• Annotations — text, highlight, and redaction tools with undo/redo
• Destructive redaction — permanently blacks out sensitive information
• Image cleanup — denoise, sharpen and fix lighting with one tap
• Encrypted storage — all files encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device
• Biometric lock — fingerprint or face unlock
• Folder organisation — one-level folders with rename and delete
• Full-text search — find any document by its content
• Export formats — PDF, JPEG, PNG, TXT, encrypted ZIP

PRIVACY
ScanVault never reads your documents. Everything is processed on your device using on-device ML Kit. No data is uploaded without your explicit consent.

PERMISSIONS
• Camera — required to scan documents
• Biometric — optional, for app lock
